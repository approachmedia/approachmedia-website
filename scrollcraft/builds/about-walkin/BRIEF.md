# BRIEF — approachmedia.in /about rebuild ("about-walkin")

Interviewed on the four structural forks via in-session questions, answered by
the owner on 2026-08-28. The remaining interview answers are derived from the
owner's request text ("web flow based on story telling in our about us page",
three uploaded walkthrough clips "for hero or inner section") and from the
copy the owner already ships on approachmedia.in. Marked per-answer below.

## The eight answers

1. **Vibe** (derived from the site + request): engineered, cinematic, confident,
   dark, real. References: their own live site (dark ground, blue-to-green
   accent, Space Grotesk); an expo hall after the crowd arrives.
2. **The journey, in their words** (derived from their existing About copy):
   what we do ("We design unique exhibition stalls so that your brand is
   understood, experienced, and remembered") → why it matters ("Exhibitions are
   high-investment environments… leave a lasting impression within moments";
   "In an ocean of lookalikes, those who stand apart will survive") → how
   (their 7-stage process) → scale (23+ years, 6000+ stalls, 14+ countries,
   500+ exhibitions a year) → book a consultation.
3. **Energy curve** (derived): calm arrival → tightening → curious → quiet →
   loud peak → calm resolve.
4. **Feeling + the one moment** (ANSWERED): peak = **the scale reveal** —
   "a wall of your real portfolio photos assembling under the scroll".
5. **One thing no site does** (derived from 4): the portfolio installs itself
   the way a stall gets built: panel by panel, and the tally is the wall.
6. **Range** (ANSWERED): **premium dark, matches the site**. The About page is
   the flagship room of the same building.
7. **Structure** (ANSWERED): **one continuous walk-in**. Note: delivered as a
   filmic one-shot, not worldflight; the three clips are separate handheld
   shots that cannot satisfy the worldflight seam law, and there is no
   generation budget for connector legs. The walk-in is narrative (approach →
   inside → lounge), not a literal unbroken camera.
8. **Assets** (ANSWERED): the three uploaded clips as-is (960×454, owner's own
   watermark, one project: the Bondzil stall) + the four real project photos in
   the repo + frames pulled from the clips. No generation; KIE key not set.

## The feeling curve

```
1  Recognition  you are on the show floor; a stall ahead on the aisle, moving under your hand   (scrub, ch1)
2  Tension      what a few seconds of attention costs, named in their own lines                 (pin, type states)
3  Curiosity    inside the build: fabrication, AV, flow, called out where they sit in frame     (scrub, ch2)
4  Confidence   the method, 7 stages, compressed and factual — the authored quiet               (flow list)
5  Awe   PEAK   24 years install themselves: the wall builds tile by tile, the tally runs       (pin + install wall + count)
6  Resolve      the lounge; sit down; one line, one action                                      (flow close, holds)
```

Authored silence: act 4 is deliberately the flattest thing on the page, a plain
list on a plain ground, so the wall has something to be a change from.

## The peak

Act 5. Sentence a visitor would say: **"the whole portfolio built itself into a
wall while a counter ran to six thousand."** Largest span on the page (3.4vh
against 2.4 for the hero).

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where **you walk down an expo aisle into a real stall, and then
24 years of stalls build themselves into a wall under your scroll.**

## Signature move

**The install wall.** Photo tiles fly in from the frame edges and mortar into a
grid, one after another, each with a settle — panels being installed on a
stall, driven per-tile from the peak act's `--sc-p` in page CSS. The running
tally is wired to the same scroll, so the number and the wall complete
together. Engine untouched.

## Numbers

All figures are the site's existing claims, verbatim: since 2002, 23+ years,
6000+ stalls, 14+ countries, 500+ exhibitions a year. Nothing invented.

## Copy rules in force (owner's standing constraints)

Never: "premier", "trusted", "leading", "world-class", "passionate". No em
dashes visible (scrollcraft rule); where the site's own lines carry one, it is
replaced with a period or colon and flagged in the report. No new factual
claims.
