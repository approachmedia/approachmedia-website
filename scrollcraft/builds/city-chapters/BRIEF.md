The 15 city landing pages (`/exhibition-stall-design-hyderabad` and 14 siblings),
all rendered by one shared `CityPageTemplate`.

Interviewed, not self-authored. The owner answered four questions in one pass;
the remaining four are carried from this session's standing direction and are
marked *(carried)* below.

## The eight answers

**1. Vibe, three to five words.** *(carried)* Dark, structural, credible,
Indian-industrial. References from this session's approved work: the service
pages' full-screen build footage, and the About walk-in.

**2. The scroll journey, section by section.** *(carried, from the page itself)*
The page already states its own order and it is not mine to re-sequence: city
claim, why the city matters, what gets a brand noticed, the venues, the
services, the sectors, **the projects**, why us, the process, questions, ask.

**3. The energy curve.** *(carried)* Calm and factual at the top, one loud
moment on the show floor, quiet through the reference material, and the build
sequence as the thing that lands.

**4. How should someone feel, stage by stage, and the ONE moment.**
*(carried; the peak is the owner's approved "concept I like" applied to this
page's own content.)* See the feeling curve below.

**5. One thing no other site does.** *(carried)* Every stall builder's site
says "we handle the process" over seven numbered cards. Nobody draws the stall.

**6. How far from premium-minimal.** **"Match the service pages"** — the
approved full-screen-video-and-text pattern plus scroll-driven text reveals.
Answered verbatim.

**7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?** Not asked as such; answered by
the scope answer. The page is a reference document with an untouchable section
in its middle, which forecloses one unbroken world. **Distinct chapters.**

**8. What assets already exist.** Answered: **the unused clips in `clips/`, the
portfolio project photos, and motion-only sections** — all three selected, so a
mix. No city-specific footage exists for any of the 15 cities.

**Plus two scope answers, asked because they could not be inferred:**

## The problem this page actually has

Five of its thirteen sections — Stand Out, Services, Industries, Why Us,
Process — are the **same centred card grid**, alternating between two
background colours. That is the skill's own failure case: five sections that
behave identically are one section shown five times. Everything below follows
from fixing that, not from adding motion.

## Feeling curve

| Chapter | Feeling | Caused by |
|---|---|---|
| Title page | Located | The city's name set as a title page, no media, nothing moving but the type |

| 05 Sectors | Breadth | A dense specimen index, small type, high count, arriving fast |
| Portfolio | *(untouched)* | The owner's section, left exactly as it is |

| 08 Questions | Settled | Quiet, plain, the accordion untouched |

**Authored silence:** chapters 05 and 06 are deliberately quiet and fast. They
carry the page's densest reference copy and they exist to make chapter 07 land.
A verification pass must not read them as dead scroll.

## The peak

> "It's the site where the exhibition stand draws itself out of an empty floor
> plan while you read how they build it, and then packs itself away again."

Lives in **chapter 07, The build**, and gets the largest span on the page.

## The tell-someone sentence

*It's the site where the stand builds itself as you scroll through how it's
built.*

## Hard constraints carried into this build

- The Portfolio chapter is not to be touched.
- No copy edits. Every heading, paragraph, list item, link and FAQ answer stays
  byte-identical: these are the site's local-SEO pages and the copy is the asset.
- Real figures only. No invented numbers, no counters over numbers the page does
  not already state.
- Photographs only from the owner's own projects and venues, already on the site.
- Never the words premier, trusted, leading, world-class, passionate in anything
  authored here. (The existing "Premier Venue" badge is the page's own copy and
  is left alone — reported, not silently changed.)

## Revision: the owner's own process animation

The peak chapter was originally my hand-drawn plan that inked itself in a pinned
act. The owner then supplied `Exhibition_Stand_Process_Animation.zip`, a Claude
Design handoff of the same idea built properly: an isometric stand that assembles
itself over seven stages beside a stage list, with a truck arriving at logistics.
That replaces the drawing. The peak, the feeling and the tell-someone sentence
are unchanged; only the mechanism is now theirs rather than mine.

Two deliberate departures from the handoff, both recorded in the component:

- the stage copy is each page's own `process` data, not the handoff's hard-coded
  list, because that list names Mumbai and these are per-city pages;
- the eyebrow and heading use the site's chapter styles so the section matches
  its neighbours.

The act is now a normal flow chapter rather than a pinned act: the visual is
sticky beside the list, offset by `--hdr` so it clears the site's sticky header.
Getting that offset wrong hid the top of the graphic under the nav, which is the
one defect this revision's verification pass caught.

## Revision: sector photographs come from the whole portfolio

"Industries we serve" now draws its photographs from every published project,
not the city's own, because the industry experience is what the section claims
and it is not city-bound. Floor of 36 sq m on stall area, one project used once,
and a card stays text-only where no genuine industry match exists rather than
being filled with a project that does not belong to that sector.
