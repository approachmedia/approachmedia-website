# The Plastics Exporter's Quarter: Plastivision + ArabPlast 2027, One Design Language

For India's plastics industry, early 2027 is a two-act quarter: **Plastivision India, 21–25 January at Bombay Exhibition Centre, Mumbai** — the domestic stage — and **ArabPlast, 24–26 March at Dubai World Trade Centre** — the 18th edition of the Gulf's plastics show, where Indian machinery and material exporters are regulars. Two months apart, two markets, and for many companies two halves of the same growth plan.

Run them as two unrelated projects and you pay for everything twice. Run them as one brief with two outputs and the maths — and the brand — compound. Here's the two-market plan.

## One design language, two correct stands

**Same identity, different jobs.** The Mumbai stand sells to Indian processors and brand buyers; the Dubai stand sells export capability to Gulf, African and CIS buyers. The brand system — colours, fascia logic, product-display vocabulary — stays constant; the messaging layer swaps: domestic range and service story in January, export reach and certifications in March ([the export-stall rules](/blog/gulfood-2027-indian-exhibitors-guide) apply at ArabPlast in full).

**Design once, adapt twice.** Briefing both shows together lets the designer resolve the hard decisions once — hierarchy, display system, lighting logic — then fit each venue's plot and rules. That's real savings in design cost, faster approvals, and a buyer who met you in Mumbai recognizing you in Dubai in half a second. (It's the advice we gave [Greater Noida's multi-show exhibitors](/blog/india-expo-mart-greater-noida-exhibitor-guide); across borders it works even harder.)

**Different build regimes, one accountable team.** Mumbai runs on the NESCO playbook ([venue guide](/blog/bombay-exhibition-centre-nesco-exhibitor-guide)); Dubai runs on DWTC's registered-contractor model ([the Dubai mechanics](/blog/exhibition-stand-cost-dubai-indian-exhibitors)). Design-in-India with turnkey delivery in both markets keeps one owner across the pair — we build for NESCO from our Ahmedabad workshop and deliver in 14+ countries including the UAE.

## The timeline that makes it work

The pairing has one catch: the deadlines aren't two months apart, because international lead time is longer. Plastivision's custom window closes in late November; ArabPlast's three-month runway starts converging by late December. **Brief both before the year ends** — this article publishes on the last sensible day to say that — and the quarter runs as one program instead of two scrambles. ## FAQ

**When are Plastivision and ArabPlast 2027?**
Plastivision: 21–25 January, NESCO Mumbai. ArabPlast: 24–26 March, Dubai World Trade Centre (18th edition).

**Can the same stand design serve both shows?**
The same design language, yes — one brand system adapted to each plot and venue regime, with domestic messaging in Mumbai and export messaging in Dubai. Identical structures rarely transfer; identities should.

**What's the saving in briefing both together?**
One design-resolution cycle instead of two, faster approvals, parallel production planning — and compounded recall with buyers who attend both markets.

**When must both be booked?**
Plastivision by late November 2026 (6–8 weeks); ArabPlast's international runway means starting by late December. One year-end brief covers the pair.
