# Windergy India 2026: Stall Planning When Your Product Is 100 Metres Tall

Wind energy has the most extreme version of a problem every industrial exhibitor knows: the product doesn't fit in the hall. A machinery exhibitor can bring the machine. A wind exhibitor brings… what, exactly?

## Solving the scale problem

### Show the piece, imply the whole

When the full product is impossible, the credible fragment wins: a blade-root section, a gearbox, a bearing assembly, a nacelle component — real hardware at real scale, staged like the engineering it is. One substantial physical exhibit beats ten posters. Component and service suppliers have it easier here than OEMs, and should lean into it: your actual product on a plinth with disciplined lighting is the whole strategy.

### Scale models and immersive media do the rest

For OEMs and project developers, precision scale models plus large-format screens carry the story — turbine ranges, wind-farm layouts, installation sequences. This is where AV earns its budget line: a well-produced installation film or interactive project map communicates scale no stall structure can. Plan screens, sightlines and content *before* stall design starts — our [AV & technology integration](/services/av-technology-integration) service exists for exactly this sequencing.

### The conference crowd is half the point

Windergy pairs the exhibition with a conference — policy people, project developers, financiers move between the two. That audience wants a conversation, not a product tour: meeting space with power, privacy and coffee converts them. Design the stall to serve both the hall visitor (visual, fast) and the conference delegate (seated, slow).
