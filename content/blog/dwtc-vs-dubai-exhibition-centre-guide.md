# DWTC vs Dubai Exhibition Centre: Dubai's Two Venues, Explained for Exhibitors

For twenty years, "exhibiting in Dubai" meant one address: **Dubai World Trade Centre (DWTC)**, downtown on Sheikh Zayed Road. It now means two. **Dubai Exhibition Centre (DEC) at Expo City** — the district built for Expo 2020, out toward Al Maktoum airport on the city's far side — has become the second pole, and organizers are actively moving and splitting shows between them.

For Indian exhibitors the confusion is practical, not academic: book the wrong hotel cluster, brief the wrong delivery route, or assume last year's venue, and your show starts with avoidable friction. Here's the sorting guide.

Three patterns stand out: legacy flagships still anchor at DWTC; some majors have relocated outright to DEC (Automechanika being the clean example); and the biggest events now span both at once — [WHX Dubai](/blog/whx-dubai-2027-indian-exhibitors-guide) and Gulfood 2027 each run as two-site shows.

## What the venue choice changes for you

**Geography first.** DWTC sits in central Dubai's business district; Expo City sits on the city's southwestern edge, nearer Al Maktoum (DWC) than Dubai International (DXB). That decides hotel cluster, commute direction and — for stand builds — delivery routing and crew travel time. A split-venue show adds the cross-town problem we mapped for [CPHI & PMEC India](/blog/cphi-pmec-india-2026-split-venue-guide): if your buyers move between sites, your meeting slots should respect the crossing.

**Rules travel with the venue.** Both venues run the registered-contractor, drawing-approval model we described in the [Dubai cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stand-cost-dubai-indian-exhibitors) — but as separate operations with their own processes and build windows. A builder experienced at one isn't automatically current at the other; ask the question directly.

**Never assume continuity.** The habit that bites exhibitors is booking on last edition's memory. Automechanika regulars heading to DWTC this November would be at the wrong end of the city. Confirm the venue on the official site the day you book space, and re-confirm when the exhibitor manual arrives.

## FAQ

**Are DWTC and Dubai Exhibition Centre the same place?**
No — DWTC is the long-established complex in central Dubai; Dubai Exhibition Centre is at Expo City on the city's southwestern side. They are separate venues with separate processes.

**Which venue is Arab Health at now?**
Its successor, WHX Dubai 2027, runs across both venues at once (25–28 January 2027) — your hall allocation determines your site; confirm early.

**Has Automechanika Dubai moved?**
Yes — the 2026 edition (10–12 November) runs at Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City, having moved from DWTC.

**Does the venue change the cost of exhibiting?**
The cost drivers are the same model ([our Dubai cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stand-cost-dubai-indian-exhibitors)); logistics, routes and build windows differ by venue, so quotes should always be venue-specific.

**Can one Indian partner handle builds at either venue?**
Yes, if their UAE execution covers both venues' contractor processes — ask specifically about recent experience at the venue your show uses.
