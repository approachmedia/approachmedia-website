# IMTEX 2027: Stall Planning for a Seven-Day Machine-Tool Marathon

Most Indian trade shows run three days. India ITME pushed six. **IMTEX runs seven — 21 to 27 January 2027, with Tooltech alongside, at BIEC in Bengaluru** — making the machine-tool industry's flagship, organized by IMTMA, the longest haul on the exhibition calendar. Add the defining feature of the show — machines running live, cutting metal, all week — and the stall brief is unlike any other.

Published ten weeks out, this guide lands at the moment the big decisions are still all available, including the double-decker question.

## Seven days changes the engineering of your stall

Everything we wrote about six-day endurance in our [India ITME guide](/blog/india-itme-2026-exhibitor-guide) applies with one more day of wear: finishes specified for traffic, lighting with service access, flooring that survives a week under industrial footfall, consumables stocked double, and a back-of-house that works like a small facility. Staff rotations stop being optional at seven days — plan the roster like a shift system, and brief the team to recognize returning visitors, because machine-tool buyers routinely inspect twice before they talk numbers.

## The running-machine stall

IMTEX's floor is working machinery, which makes the stall part engineering project:

- **Power and foundations first.** Declared loads, machine positioning sequenced with the venue, and the stall designed around the machine's installed position — the same arrives-first discipline from our [bauma CONEXPO guide](/blog/bauma-conexpo-india-2026-exhibitor-guide), sustained for a week.
- **Chips, coolant and safety clearance.** Live cutting means mess management and audience separation designed in, not taped off on day one.
- **Sightlines to the action.** A running machine is the strongest crowd magnet in exhibiting; arrange the viewing line so watchers don't block your meeting zone or your neighbours' aisle.
- **The conversation happens seated.** Machine-tool purchases are long-cycle capital decisions; the stall needs a documented quiet zone where specifications come out — glass-walled if budget allows.

## Bengaluru logistics, planned from anywhere

BIEC sits on Tumkur Road at the city's northern edge; we cover the venue fully in our BIEC guide publishing next month. For exhibitors shipping from elsewhere in India, the rule we build on is unchanged: fabricate and 100% mock-up test in the workshop, dispatch finished, and treat the venue build window as assembly, not construction — on-time delivery guaranteed in writing.

## FAQ

**When and where is IMTEX 2027?**
21–27 January 2027 — seven days — at BIEC, Bengaluru, with Tooltech 2027 alongside. Organized by IMTMA.

**What's different about exhibiting for seven days?**
Wear and people. Specify endurance finishes, double the consumables, plan staff rotations like shifts, and design for buyers who return for a second look before committing.

**Can machines run live at IMTEX stalls?**
Live demonstration is central to the show — subject to declared power, positioning sequence, safety clearances and the exhibitor manual's rules. Declare everything early.

**Is a double-decker stall worth it at IMTEX?**
If you need serious meeting capacity, few shows justify one better — seven days of long-cycle conversations. The 10–12-week runway means deciding by late November 2026.

**What does an IMTEX stall cost?**
The India range applies — ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sq metre custom, depending on design ([cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india)); machine logistics and seven-day endurance specs shape where you land in it.
