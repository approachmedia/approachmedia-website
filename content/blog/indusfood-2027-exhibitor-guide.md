# IndusFood 2027: Exhibiting Where the Buyers Fly In

Most Indian food shows sell to India. **IndusFood sells India to the world** — the Trade Promotion Council of India's export-focused F&B show, running **8–10 January 2027 at India Expo Mart, Greater Noida**, built around international buyer delegations meeting Indian producers.

That inversion decides everything about the stall. We've written before about what export-platform shows demand — it was the heart of our iPHEX delivery — and IndusFood is the food industry's version of the same brief. Published eight weeks out, this guide lands exactly on the booking line.

## The export-show stall, food edition

International food buyers walk an aisle asking three questions, and the stall should answer all three before your team speaks:

**"Where do you already ship?"** Market flags, export-range panels, retail-shelf mockups of your international packaging — reach made visible. At our iPHEX build this was the single most-repeated design element across eight stalls, because it works.

**"Are you certified for my shelves?"** Food-safety and market certifications belong in the headline zone, not the brochure — for an importer they're the gate, not the garnish.

**"What does it taste like?"** Sampling is the closing argument. Plan the tasting line as infrastructure: service counters, power and utilities declared at booking, hygiene-visual presentation (the design language from our [Anuga FoodTec guide](/blog/anuga-foodtec-india-2026-exhibitor-guide) applies), and queue space that doesn't strangle your own displays.

And because delegation buyers run schedules, not strolls: a seated meeting zone with your catalogue, price structure and export documentation within reach converts the fifteen minutes you get.

## January at India Expo Mart

IndusFood opens the venue's densest quarter — IFEX, ELECRAMA and GTE follow within ten weeks ([the venue guide](/blog/india-expo-mart-greater-noida-exhibitor-guide) has the ground rules, and [our Q1 calendar](/blog/india-trade-shows-jan-mar-2027-calendar) the full picture). Early January shows carry a hidden schedule trap: **the build-up window lands in the first days of January, which means fabrication happens in December** — holidays, year-end factory closures and compressed logistics included. The 6–8 week rule puts your booking deadline in mid-November; for this show more than most, treat it as firm.

Our standard protects January openings specifically: fabricate and 100% mock-up test in the workshop in December, dispatch finished, assemble inside the venue window — on-time delivery guaranteed in writing.

## FAQ

**When and where is IndusFood 2027?**
8–10 January 2027 at India Expo Mart, Greater Noida — organized by the Trade Promotion Council of India.

**How is IndusFood different from other food shows?**
It's export-focused: international buyer delegations sourcing from Indian producers. The stall must present reach, certifications and range to buyers comparing countries, not just companies.

**Can I run tasting at my stall?**
Sampling is central — plan service counters, utilities and queue space as designed infrastructure, declared with the organizer at booking.

**Why is the booking deadline firmer for a January show?**
Because fabrication falls in December, with year-end closures and compressed logistics. Mid-November is the realistic custom-stall deadline for this edition.

**What does a stall at India Expo Mart cost?**
The India range applies — ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sq metre custom, depending on design ([cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india)) — quoted venue-specific.
