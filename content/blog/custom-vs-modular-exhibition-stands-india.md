# Custom vs Modular Exhibition Stands: Which Should Indian Exhibitors Choose?

Most articles on this question are written by UK and European stand companies for their markets. The logic changes in India — labour economics, show culture, and what organizers allow are different here. So here's the India answer.

## The two options, defined plainly

**Modular stands** are built from reusable system components — typically aluminium frame systems with infill panels and printed graphics. They assemble in configurations, pack into cases, and can be rented or bought.

**Custom stands** are designed and fabricated for your brand and your show — one-off structures built in a workshop, finished to your spec, and installed on site.

**Hybrid** uses a system skeleton dressed with custom-built elements — a middle path that's more common than either camp admits.

## Cost: the comparison that actually matters

In India, **custom stall design and fabrication runs ₹7,000–₹25,000 per square metre** depending on the design. Modular typically enters cheaper per show — but the comparison isn't one show. It's this:

- **Custom** — you pay for design and fabrication each time, and each time the stall is exactly what that show needs. No storage problem, no reassembly compromise, full brand control.
- **Modular owned** — higher upfront purchase, then cheaper per show *if* you exhibit often, *if* you store and maintain it, and *if* your stand size stays similar show to show. The savings are real but conditional.
- **Modular rented** — lowest commitment, most generic result. Fine for a first show or a directory-style presence; forgettable at a show where your competitors built custom.

One more India-specific point: skilled fabrication labour here is economical relative to Europe, which is exactly why custom is within reach for mid-size Indian exhibitors at budgets where a European company would be forced into modular. The UK-centric advice of "modular unless you're huge" simply imports the wrong labour economics.

## The other five comparison points

### Brand impact

Custom wins, and it isn't close. A modular stand says "we are present." A custom stall says "this is who we are." At a trade show, where a visitor gives you seconds, the structure *is* the message. In an ocean of lookalikes, a system-built stand looks like the ocean.

### Reuse and repeat shows

Modular wins on paper. In practice, audit yourself: will you exhibit 4+ times a year at similar stand sizes, and will someone own the storage, refurbishment and logistics between shows? If yes, modular ownership maths works. If your real pattern is 1–3 shows a year at varying sizes, custom-per-show usually costs less than owning hardware you fight with.

### Timeline

Modular is faster to deploy — days, not weeks. Custom needs six to eight weeks from brief to show. If you booked late, modular (or a hybrid) may be the only honest option — a rushed custom build is the worst of both worlds.

### Design freedom vs venue flexibility

Custom gives total freedom for that show's exact space — corner, island, odd dimensions, double-height. Modular flexes across *different* spaces but within the system's vocabulary: mostly straight lines, standard heights, panel rhythms.

### What happens after the show

Custom stalls are typically dismantled — you're not paying to warehouse anything. Modular must be packed, transported, stored and maintained, and those costs hide outside the quote you compared.

## A simple decision rule

- **1–3 shows a year, brand matters, budget from ₹7,000/sqm up** → custom
- **4+ shows a year, similar sizes, someone owns logistics** → modular or hybrid
- **Booked late or first-ever show** → modular/hybrid now; plan custom for the next one
- **Flagship show of your year** → custom, whatever you do at the small shows

## FAQ

**Which is cheaper, custom or modular?**
Per show, modular usually enters cheaper. Across your real exhibition calendar — including storage, refurbishment and the leads a stand generates — the answer depends on how often you exhibit and how much brand impact matters. Custom in India runs ₹7,000–₹25,000/sqm depending on design.

**Can a modular stand look premium?**
Good graphics and lighting help, but a system stand reads as a system stand up close. Hybrid builds close some of the gap.

**How far in advance does a custom stall need to be booked?**
Six to eight weeks before the show; ten to twelve for a double-decker.

**Can I reuse parts of a custom stall?**
Selected elements — counters, lightboxes, product displays — can be designed for reuse. Tell your designer upfront; retrofitting reusability doesn't work.

**What if I exhibit in India and abroad?**
Rules and logistics differ by country. We deliver in 14+ countries — the design approach travels, the build strategy adapts per market.
