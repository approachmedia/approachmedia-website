# METEC + wire + Tube India 2026: One Build Window, Four Shows

India's metals world converges on one Mumbai floor this winter. From **30 November to 2 December 2026**, Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO) hosts four co-located Messe Düsseldorf shows at once: **METEC India** (metallurgy), **wire India**, **Tube India**, and **India Essen Welding & Cutting**.

For exhibitors, co-location at this scale is both the draw and the challenge: the entire supply chain walks the same halls — and so does everyone you compete with. Publishing ten weeks out, this guide lands in your ideal booking window.

## Exhibiting inside a cluster

### Your hall is your context — know it before designing

Each show anchors its own exhibitor community, but visitors flow across all four. Where your stand sits determines who walks past by default and who has to seek you out. Before design starts, understand your hall's audience: a welding-consumables stand surrounded by metallurgy plant suppliers has a different visibility problem than one among its direct peers. Ask the organizer for the hall plan early and design your aisle-facing message for the traffic you'll actually get.

### Category clarity beats brand volume

With four shows' worth of exhibitors, visitors triage by category first: "wire dies", "induction furnaces", "orbital welding". Your aisle-facing headline should answer *what you are* in three seconds; the brand story works on whoever stops. This is the cluster version of advice we've given for [NESCO's dense season](/blog/bombay-exhibition-centre-nesco-exhibitor-guide) — at a four-show event it's not optional.

### Heavy product, German-fair discipline

Metals shows mean real hardware on display — and Messe Düsseldorf runs its Indian shows with the same structured exhibitor-manual culture as its German parent fairs: drawing approvals, defined build windows, declared weights and power. The manual arrives; it goes to your builder the same day. (Our new [stand design approval guide](/blog/stand-design-approval-process-india) covers what gets submitted and why — publishing this week.)

### The flagship stakes

Many exhibitors treat this cluster as their flagship Indian outing of the cycle. If that's you, budget like it: this is the show where finish quality, a real meeting area and disciplined product display earn two years of credibility — and where a tired stand costs the same two years.
