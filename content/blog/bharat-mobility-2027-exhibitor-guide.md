# Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2027: Six Days, Two Venues, One Plan

India's largest expo stage returns: **Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2027 — the third edition of the government-anchored mobility show — runs 4–9 February 2027 across two venues at once: Bharat Mandapam in central Delhi and Yashobhoomi in Dwarka** (the Yashobhoomi leg runs 4–7 February). Six days at the main venue, OEMs and the component ecosystem together, and the highest ambient stall standard in the country.

If the auto and mobility supply chain is your market, this is the quarter's defining commitment — and at eleven weeks out, this guide publishes exactly when the big decisions are still open.

## The dual-venue decision comes first

Like [CPHI & PMEC India](/blog/cphi-pmec-india-2026-split-venue-guide) — the split-venue playbook applies — your first planning fact is **which venue your segment sits in**, because everything downstream (build rules, logistics, hotel, visitor profile) follows it. OEM showcases and component zones are distributed across the two sites; your space confirmation tells you your ground. We've mapped both venues: [Bharat Mandapam](/blog/bharat-mandapam-exhibitor-guide) and [Yashobhoomi](/blog/yashobhoomi-iicc-delhi-exhibitor-guide). Note the asymmetric dates — the Yashobhoomi leg closes two days earlier — which matters for staffing plans and dismantling slots.

## Exhibiting next to the biggest stands in India

**Calibrate, don't compete.** OEM pavilions here are experiential productions; a component maker trying to out-spectacle them burns budget for nothing. The winning mid-size play is the one that works at every flagship: one aisle-readable statement of what you make, product displayed as engineering, and meeting capacity for the OEM procurement conversations that are the real reason you're here.

**Six days is an endurance spec.** The ITME/IMTEX rules apply: durable finishes, serviceable lighting, double consumables, staff rotations ([the seven-day version](/blog/imtex-2027-exhibitor-guide) of this argument, two weeks before this show's deadline cycle).

**Government-event formality.** Expect strict manuals, early drawing deadlines, security layering and VIP walkthroughs — the [Bharat Mandapam ground rules](/blog/bharat-mandapam-exhibitor-guide) at their fullest. The delegation moment rewards one legible headline; the security reality rewards schedule buffers.

## FAQ

**When and where is Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2027?**
4–9 February 2027, across Bharat Mandapam and Yashobhoomi in New Delhi; the Yashobhoomi leg runs 4–7 February. Third edition, government-anchored.

**Which venue will my company exhibit at?**
Segment zoning across the two venues is set by the organizer — confirm your allocation early; it determines rules, logistics and audience.

**How should a component maker's stall compete with OEM pavilions?**
It shouldn't try. Category clarity, engineering-grade product display and real meeting capacity convert procurement visitors; spectacle is the OEMs' game.

**When must I book a stall builder?**
Custom: by mid-December 2026 (and December is fabrication's busiest month — earlier is safer). Double-decker: within about two weeks of this article's date.

**What does a stall at this show cost?**
The India range applies — ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sq metre custom, depending on design ([cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india)); flagship ambitions and six-day specs shape where in the range you land.
