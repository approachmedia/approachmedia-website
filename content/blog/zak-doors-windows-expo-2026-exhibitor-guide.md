## What a fenestration stall must do

### Working samples beat pictures — by a mile

This is a touch-and-operate industry. Full-size operating samples — a sliding system that glides, a casement that closes with the right sound, a hardware wall at hand height — are the display strategy. Build frames that let buyers operate products without queuing, and brace them for three days of enthusiastic slamming.

### Your stall's finish is read as your factory's finish

At most shows, build quality is a background signal; here it is practically a demonstration. Crisp shadow gaps, dead-straight verticals, flush junctions — fenestration buyers notice millimetres professionally. This is why our workshop standard is a 100% mock-up test before dispatch: at this show, "almost aligned" is a visible defect.

### Cross-sections tell the technical story

Profile cut-sections — thermal breaks, chamber structures, gasket lines — displayed at eye level with clean lighting do the engineering talk before your team does. The aluminium-industry stalls we covered in our [Aluminium Bharat guide](/blog/aluminium-bharat-2026-exhibitor-guide) proved the same logic: when the product is a profile, the specimen is the star.

### Plan for the dealer conversation

Fenestration shows draw channel buyers looking for dealership terms. That conversation needs a seated corner with documentation — not a standing chat by a display. Protect meeting space in the layout.
