# Exhibitor's Guide to BIEC, Bengaluru

South India's exhibition anchor is **BIEC — the Bangalore International Exhibition Centre — on Tumkur Road at Bengaluru's north-western edge**, home ground of IMTEX and a steady calendar of machine-tool, technology and industry shows. If your 2027 plans include the South, odds are they include this venue; here's the planning view.

| Show | Dates | Industry | Booking window status (custom = 6–8 weeks) |
|---|---|---|---|
| IMTEX 2027 & Tooltech 2027 | 21–27 January 2027 (7 days) | Machine tools & manufacturing | Closing — decide now ([our IMTEX guide](/blog/imtex-2027-exhibitor-guide)) |

Recent editions we've covered at this venue include LASER World of PHOTONICS INDIA and the HBLF Show (both September 2026) and DIDAC India (November 2026) — the pattern is clear: precision manufacturing, technology and education shows treat BIEC as their southern base, with IMTEX as the flagship tenant.

## What to plan for at BIEC

**The Tumkur Road reality.** The venue sits at the city's edge, a serious commute from central Bengaluru's hotels and the airport on the opposite side. Visitors plan BIEC as a destination trip — which, as at Greater Noida, means the people who arrive are qualified by effort; build for conversion, not footfall gimmicks. For exhibitors, hotel choice near the northern corridor saves the team hours across a long show.

**Long shows live here.** IMTEX's seven-day format makes BIEC the home of endurance exhibiting — finishes specified for wear, staff rotations, a working back-of-house ([the full endurance argument](/blog/imtex-2027-exhibitor-guide)). Even at three-day BIEC shows, the machinery-heavy audience rewards the same robustness.

**Manuals rule, as everywhere.** Hall allocations, heights, power and build windows come from each show's exhibitor manual — BIEC's organizers (IMTMA's own shows above all) run structured, deadline-firm processes ([how approvals work](/blog/stand-design-approval-process-india)). Manual to your builder on day one.

**Delivering to Bengaluru.** Our model is unchanged by geography: fabricate and 100% mock-up test in the Ahmedabad workshop, dispatch finished, assemble inside the window — on-time delivery guaranteed in writing. Long-haul delivery rewards the stall that arrives complete; venue build windows are for assembly, not construction.

## Planning your stall

The standing rules: six to eight weeks for custom, ten to twelve for double-deckers ([rules](/blog/double-decker-stall-rules-india)), venue-specific fixed-price quotes, and for IMTEX specifically — the seven-day flagship — the booking window is closing as this page publishes.

## FAQ

**Where is BIEC?**
On Tumkur Road, at Bengaluru's north-western edge — plan hotels along the northern corridor and treat airport transfers as cross-city trips.

**What's the next major show at BIEC?**
IMTEX 2027 with Tooltech, 21–27 January 2027 — seven days, the machine-tool flagship. Its custom-stall booking window closes in mid-December.

**Does Approach Media deliver stalls in Bengaluru?**
Yes — Bengaluru is among our service cities; stalls are fabricated and fully mock-up tested in Ahmedabad and delivered finished.

**Are BIEC's stall rules standard across shows?**
No — each organizer's exhibitor manual sets heights, approvals and build windows per show. The manual is the governing document everywhere.

**What does a stall at BIEC cost?**
The India range applies — ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sq metre custom, depending on design ([cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india)) — plus Bengaluru delivery logistics in a venue-specific quote.
