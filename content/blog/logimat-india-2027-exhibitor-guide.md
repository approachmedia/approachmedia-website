# LogiMAT India 2027: Exhibiting Products That Move

Most stalls display objects. Intralogistics stalls display *motion* — an AGV tracing its loop, a shuttle climbing racking, a conveyor sorting live. **LogiMAT India 2027, Messe Stuttgart's intralogistics and warehousing show, runs 8–10 February at Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO), Mumbai**, and the exhibitors who win it are the ones who treat moving demonstrations as the stall's architecture, not an accessory.

## Designing for motion

**The demo loop is the floor plan.** A running AGV or live conveyor needs dedicated, protected floor area — which means the stall is designed around the demonstration path first and the walls second. Decide the demo before the design brief goes out ([the brief template](/blog/stall-design-brief-template) has a field for exactly this), because retrofitting motion into a finished layout is the expensive way.

**Safety clearance is audience design.** Moving equipment plus crowds equals barriers, sightlines and standing room engineered together. The viewing edge is your best real estate — put the explainer graphics and a team member there, where the questions happen.

**Height sells racking.** Warehouse systems are vertical products; a show-legal racking bay, properly lit and loaded with real totes, demonstrates more than any render wall. Heights and any rigging go through the manual's approvals ([how that works](/blog/stand-design-approval-process-india)) — submit early.

**Power and network, declared.** Live automation needs clean power and often connectivity — organizer services with deadlines, not build-day improvisations. Our [AV & technology integration](/services/av-technology-integration) work exists for the control-screen layer: dashboards beside machines, showing the system thinking.

## FAQ

**When and where is LogiMAT India 2027?**
8–10 February 2027 at Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO), Mumbai — organized by Messe Stuttgart India.

**Can AGVs and conveyors run live at the stall?**
Live demonstration is the show's core — design the stall around the demo path, with safety clearance and declared power/network through the exhibitor manual.

**How tall can a racking display be?**
Heights are set per show and position by the manual, with approvals for anything unusual — submit drawings early rather than designing to a guessed limit.

**When should I book my stall builder?**
By mid-December 2026 (6–8 weeks out) — and earlier if you're also building for IFEX the same week, which is a parallel-capacity question for your fabricator.

**What does a LogiMAT stall cost?**
The India range — ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sq metre custom, depending on design ([cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india)); demo infrastructure is quoted per project.
