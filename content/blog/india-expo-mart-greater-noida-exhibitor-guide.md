# Exhibitor's Guide to India Expo Mart, Greater Noida

Ask three exhibitors where their show is and you'll hear three names: "India Expo Mart", "India Expo Centre", "IEML". **They're the same complex** — run by India Exposition Mart Ltd in Greater Noida — and it's where a striking share of India's big B2B calendar lands. This September it hosted bauma CONEXPO INDIA; through December, three more major shows follow.

Here's the venue, its remaining 2026 calendar, and how to plan a stall for it.

Two calendar quirks to hold in mind. In October, two shows share the venue on the same dates — REI and The Battery Show — so build-up periods, deliveries and hotel demand all double. In November, PMEC runs here while its sister show CPHI India runs across NCR at Yashobhoomi — the split-venue week our [dedicated guide](/blog/cphi-pmec-india-2026-split-venue-guide) unpacks.

## Why organizers keep choosing this venue

The complex is purpose-built for large trade fairs and handles machinery-heavy shows routinely — construction equipment in September, textile machinery in December. For exhibitors that means the venue is practised at heavy move-ins and multi-day builds. It does not mean rules are standard: **each organizer configures halls, heights, deadlines and build windows per show**, and the exhibitor manual for your specific event remains the only document that governs your stall.

## Planning a stall for a Greater Noida show

### Location logistics, stated plainly

Greater Noida sits at the eastern edge of Delhi NCR. For visitors it's a planned trip, not a drop-in — which slightly changes stall strategy: people who made the journey are qualified by effort, so conversion infrastructure (meeting space, follow-up capture) matters more than footfall gimmicks. For exhibitors, hotel and travel booking early matters, especially for the double-booked October dates.

### Deliveries arrive finished or not at all

Long-distance delivery to a tightly-scheduled venue rewards the builder whose stall arrives complete. Our discipline: fabricate and 100% mock-up test in the 30,000 sq ft Ahmedabad workshop, then dispatch — with on-time delivery guaranteed in writing. On a shared build window (October's two-show week especially), "we'll finish on site" is how stalls miss opening.

### The season compounds

Exhibiting at more than one show here this season — REI in October, PMEC in November, ITME in December? Brief one design language across them. Reused design thinking (not necessarily reused structures) cuts design cost and builds recognition with the NCR trade audience that attends multiple shows.

## FAQ

**Are India Expo Mart and India Expo Centre the same place?**
Yes — one complex in Greater Noida, run by India Exposition Mart Ltd (IEML). Show websites use the names interchangeably.

**Which shows run at India Expo Mart for the rest of 2026?**
Confirmed as of 15 August 2026: REI Expo and The Battery Show India (both 22–24 Oct), PMEC India (24–26 Nov) and INDIA ITME (4–9 Dec). bauma CONEXPO INDIA ran 15–18 Sep.

**How early should I book a stall builder for a show here?**
Six to eight weeks for custom, ten to twelve for a double-decker. For PMEC and ITME the ideal window is open now; for REI/Battery Show it's closing.

**Does the venue have standard stall rules?**
No — each show's organizer sets its own hall plans, height limits and deadlines. The exhibitor manual for your show governs everything.

**Can a builder outside Delhi NCR deliver here?**
Routinely — what matters is a venue-specific quote and a build that arrives finished. Ours are fabricated and fully mock-up tested in Ahmedabad before dispatch.
