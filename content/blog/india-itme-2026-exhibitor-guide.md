## What six days does to a stall

### Durability is a budget line, not an assumption

A stall that looks sharp for three days can look tired by day five — scuffed counters, failing lighting, carpet worn at the demo queue. For a six-day show, specify finishes for endurance: laminates and flooring chosen for traffic, lighting with service access, storage stocked for double the consumables. This is exactly where mock-up testing before dispatch earns its keep — our standard on 100% of builds — because a weak joint that survives a mock-up won't be discovered by day-four footfall.

### Staff the marathon, not the sprint

Six days at full intensity burns out a three-person team. Plan rotations, a real pantry/storage area (not an afterthought corner), and seating your team can actually rest in. The stall's back-of-house is a working facility for a week — design it like one.

### Running machinery is the show

Textile buyers want to see machines run — thread moving, fabric forming. That means power provisioning declared early, vibration and noise considered in your layout, safety clearances around moving parts, and material supply for six days of demonstrations. Machine positioning is sequenced with the venue before the stall builds around it — the same discipline we described for [bauma CONEXPO's heavy exhibits](/blog/bauma-conexpo-india-2026-exhibitor-guide), sustained for twice as long.

### Mill owners buy slowly and return

ITME's audience — mill owners, technical directors, procurement teams — often visits a stand more than once across six days before a serious conversation. Design for the return visit: a meeting space that can be pre-booked, product documentation worth taking away, and a team briefed to recognize and escalate second-time visitors.

## FAQ

**When and where is India ITME 2026?**
4–9 December 2026 — six days — at India Expo Centre & Mart, Greater Noida. It's the 12th edition, organized by the India ITME Society.

**How is a six-day show different for exhibitors?**
Everything wears: finishes, lighting, consumables and people. Specify durability, plan staff rotations, and give the stall a real back-of-house.

**Can machines run live at ITME stalls?**
Live demonstrations are central to textile machinery shows — subject to power declarations, safety clearances and the exhibitor manual's rules. Declare requirements early; retrofitting power is painful.

**When should I book my stall builder for ITME?**
