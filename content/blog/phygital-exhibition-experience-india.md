# Phygital Exhibitions: Connecting Your Stall With Digital Before, During and After the Show

A three-day exhibition used to be treated as a three-day event. In 2026, the most successful exhibitors treat it as a **campaign** that starts weeks before the show and continues long after the stall comes down.

This approach is called **phygital**: joining the physical stall experience with digital touchpoints. It helps brands attract the right visitors, engage them better on the floor and turn more conversations into business.

## What does "phygital" mean for an exhibition?

Phygital simply means that your physical stall and your digital marketing work together. Your stall is not a standalone space; it's the centre of a connected journey.

That journey has three stages.

## Stage 1: Before the show

Pre-show marketing decides who walks into your stall.

- **Announce your participation** on LinkedIn, WhatsApp and email with your stall number and hall
- **Share stall previews** using renders or short walkthrough videos
- **Book meetings in advance** with key prospects and existing clients
- **Tease a launch or demo** to give people a reason to visit
- **Add the show to your website** with a dedicated landing page

Walkthrough videos and multi-angle renders are also helpful internally, so stakeholders can agree on the design before production begins.

## Stage 2: During the show

On the floor, digital tools make every interaction more useful.

- **QR codes** for catalogues, videos, price lists and brochures
- **Digital lead capture** through badge scanning or quick forms
- **Touchscreens and product configurators** for self-guided exploration
- **Live social media updates**: product launches, client visits, team moments
- **Live streaming** of demos or talks for people who couldn't attend
- **Social media walls** that show posts from visitors in real time

A phygital stall should also be designed with these in mind. Screens, QR points and lead capture stations need the right position, power and lighting.

## Stage 3: After the show

This is where many exhibitors lose value. Leads go cold if follow-up takes weeks.

- **Follow up within 24 to 48 hours** by email, WhatsApp or phone
- **Personalise messages** based on what each visitor was interested in
- **Share show highlights** on LinkedIn and your website
- **Sync leads to your CRM** and assign owners
- **Track outcomes**: meetings, quotations and closed deals from the show

## Why phygital works

- **Better visitors:** pre-show marketing brings in more relevant prospects
- **Longer reach:** content reaches people who never visited the hall
- **Better data:** you know who engaged with what
- **Measurable ROI:** leads can be traced to real business outcomes
- **Longer life:** a three-day event becomes months of marketing content

## How stall design supports a phygital strategy

Phygital isn't just a marketing plan. It affects the physical stall too:

- Screens placed at eye level and in visible spots
- QR codes printed large enough and in good light
- Power and internet planned for devices
- A clear area for photos and social media content
- Lead capture stations at natural conversation endpoints
- Branded backdrops that look good on camera

## A simple phygital checklist

**Before:**
