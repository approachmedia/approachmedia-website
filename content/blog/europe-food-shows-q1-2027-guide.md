# Three German Food Shows, Three Weeks: ISM, Fruit Logistica and BIOFACH from India

Germany's winter food circuit packs three specialist shows into twenty days: **ISM, the sweets and snacks fair, 31 January–3 February at Koelnmesse, Cologne; Fruit Logistica, fresh produce and its logistics, 3–5 February at Messe Berlin; and BIOFACH, the organic food fair, 16–19 February at Messezentrum Nürnberg.** For Indian exporters — snacks and confectionery, fresh and processed produce, and the fast-growing organic segment — the cluster is one planning problem, not three.

None of these is a general food show; each is a sharp vertical with its own buyers. The stand plan follows from which you're in — and whether you're in more than one.

Note the brutal ISM→Fruit Logistica turnaround: Cologne closes on the 3rd, Berlin opens the same day. Exhibiting at both means two stands running simultaneously in two cities — a parallel-build, parallel-staffing commitment to plan deliberately, never improvised.

## Stand logic by vertical

**ISM: the shelf test.** Confectionery buyers imagine your pack on their shelf — retail-grade presentation, lit packaging walls, and tasting as infrastructure. The [Gulfood brief](/blog/gulfood-2027-indian-exhibitors-guide) applies, compressed into German hall discipline.

**Fruit Logistica: freshness is the display.** Real produce, properly chilled and replenished, outsells any photograph — which makes utilities (power, refrigeration, daily restocking) the design's first line, declared early through the organizer. Logistics buyers also walk this show: if your story includes cold chain or packaging, give it its own legible zone.

**BIOFACH: certification is the headline.** Organic buyers verify before they taste — certifications and traceability belong at aisle height, in the headline zone, with the product story built on them. Natural materials in the stand itself reinforce the category promise the way precision framed [Eleanor's engineering brand](/blog/award-winning-exhibition-stall-design-eleanor-aluminium-bharat-2026).

**All three:** appointment-driven buyers, the European finish bar ([the Heimtextil argument](/blog/heimtextil-2027-indian-exhibitors-guide) holds), and differentiation within India's pavilion presence where one exists.

## FAQ

**Which show fits which exporter?**
ISM for sweets and snacks, Fruit Logistica for fresh produce and its logistics chain, BIOFACH for certified organic ranges. They're sharp verticals, not general food fairs.

**Can one company exhibit at ISM and Fruit Logistica both?**
Yes, but they overlap by a day in different cities — two simultaneous stands, parallel builds and split teams. Plan it as one program with explicit capacity questions to your builder.

**What's non-negotiable on a BIOFACH stand?**
Certifications at headline height — organic buyers verify before anything else — backed by traceability and a stand whose materials match the category's promise.

**Is January too late to start?**
For full custom, yes — the runway was November. Late deciders should take a disciplined system-stand upgrade this cycle and calendar the next one three months out.
