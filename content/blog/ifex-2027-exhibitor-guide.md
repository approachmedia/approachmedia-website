# IFEX 2027: Presenting Heavy Metal Precisely

Foundry products don't flatter themselves. Castings are heavy, grey and industrial — and the engineering excellence inside them is invisible unless the display works to show it. That's the design problem of **IFEX 2027 — the 23rd Indian Foundry Exhibition, running with the 75th Indian Foundry Congress — on 8–10 February 2027 at India Expo Mart, Greater Noida**, organized by the Institute of Indian Foundrymen.

## Making castings speak

**Stage the part like the engineering it is.** A machined casting on a lit plinth against a clean background reads as precision; the same part on a cloth-covered table reads as scrap. Weight-rated plinths, raking light to show surface quality, and section cuts that reveal wall thickness and integrity — the Aluminium Bharat lesson ([the specimen is the star](/blog/aluminium-bharat-2026-exhibitor-guide)) in its heaviest form.

**Declare the weights early.** Castings and foundry equipment mean floor loading, machine-first installation and venue sequencing — the [bauma discipline](/blog/bauma-conexpo-india-2026-exhibitor-guide). The exhibitor manual's weight declarations aren't paperwork; they're what lets your centrepiece onto the floor.

**The congress doubles the audience's seniority.** With the 75th Indian Foundry Congress alongside, the venue carries decision-makers between sessions and halls. A one-line aisle message (your alloy range, your casting capability, your industry) catches the delegate with twenty minutes; a seated corner with documentation converts them.

**Before-and-after beats brochures.** Raw casting → machined component → application context, in one sightline: the transformation story is the foundry industry's strongest display pattern, and it needs only three plinths and honest lighting.

## FAQ

**When and where is IFEX 2027?**
8–10 February 2027 at India Expo Mart, Greater Noida — the 23rd Indian Foundry Exhibition, held with the 75th Indian Foundry Congress. Organized by IIF.

**How should castings be displayed at a stall?**
As engineering: weight-rated lit plinths, raking light for surface quality, section cuts for integrity, and a raw-to-machined-to-application sightline that tells the transformation story.

**Are there special requirements for heavy exhibits?**
Yes — declared weights, floor-loading compliance and machine-first installation sequencing through the exhibitor manual. Declare early.

**When should I book my stall builder?**
By mid-December 2026 — the 6–8 week line, landing inside the year-end fabrication squeeze. Sooner is safer.

**What does an IFEX stall cost?**
