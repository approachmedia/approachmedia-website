# Plastivision 2027: The Plastics Show of the Season (No, Plastindia Isn't in 2027)

First, the confusion worth clearing: **there is no Plastindia in 2027.** That show runs on a multi-year cycle, its last edition was February 2026, and the next lands in 2028. The plastics industry's big Indian stage this season is **Plastivision India 2027 — the 13th edition, organized by AIPMA — running 21–25 January at Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO), Mumbai.** Five days, machinery running, the processing and packaging ecosystem in one place.

If you build your exhibition calendar on "the January plastics show," this is it. Published nine weeks out — the booking window is open and real.

## Stall planning for a five-day machinery show

**Running machines, managed properly.** Injection moulding and extrusion demos are the floor's crowd-pullers — which means declared power loads, machine-first installation sequence, safety clearance, and material supply for five days. The discipline is the one from our [bauma CONEXPO guide](/blog/bauma-conexpo-india-2026-exhibitor-guide); the duration adds the endurance rules from [ITME](/blog/india-itme-2026-exhibitor-guide): tough finishes, serviceable lighting, staff rotations.

**Granules to product, in one sightline.** The most effective plastics stalls tell the material story physically — polymer in, component out, arranged so a passing buyer reads the transformation in seconds. If you make products rather than machines, the same principle inverts: the application wall (your parts in their end-use context) does the explaining.

**Two audiences, one stall.** Plastivision draws both processors buying machinery and brand buyers sourcing products. Category clarity on the fascia sorts the traffic; a seated zone converts the capital-equipment conversations, which are long-cycle and documentation-heavy.

**NESCO in January.** The venue's compressed build windows and dense calendar are mapped in our [NESCO exhibitor's guide](/blog/bombay-exhibition-centre-nesco-exhibitor-guide); note that LogiMAT India follows at the same venue two weeks later — exhibitors doing both should brief one design language across the pair.

## FAQ

**Is there a Plastindia in 2027?**
No — Plastindia's next edition is 2028. Plastivision India 2027 (21–25 January, NESCO Mumbai) is the season's major Indian plastics show.

**When and where is Plastivision 2027?**
21–25 January 2027 — five days — at Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO), Mumbai. The 13th edition, organized by AIPMA.

**Can machines run live at the stall?**
Yes, within the exhibitor manual's rules — declare power and positioning early, and plan safety clearance and five days of demo material.

**When should I book my stall builder?**
By late November 2026 for a custom stall (6–8 weeks); double-decker decisions are due immediately (10–12 weeks).

**What does a Plastivision stall cost?**
The India range applies — ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sq metre custom, depending on design ([cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india)) — plus Mumbai logistics; insist on a venue-specific quote.
