# How Much Does Exhibition Stall Design & Fabrication Cost in India? (2026 Rates)

Every week, someone calls us with the same first question: *"What will a stall cost?"* And every directory listing gives them a range so wide it's useless.

Here's the actual answer, from 23+ years and 6000+ stalls built.

## The short answer: ₹7,000 to ₹25,000 per square metre

Custom exhibition stall design and fabrication in India costs **₹7,000 to ₹25,000 per square metre**, depending on the design of the stall.

That single sentence hides a 3.5× spread, so here's what it means at common stall sizes:

| Stall size | Area | Indicative range (design + fabrication) |
|---|---|---|
| 3m × 3m (inline) | 9 sqm | ₹63,000 – ₹2.25 lakh |
| 6m × 3m | 18 sqm | ₹1.26 lakh – ₹4.5 lakh |
| 6m × 6m | 36 sqm | ₹2.52 lakh – ₹9 lakh |
| 10m × 10m (island) | 100 sqm | ₹7 lakh – ₹25 lakh |

These are the per-sqm range multiplied out — not separate quotes. Where you land inside the range depends almost entirely on design decisions, which is the next section.

## What moves you within the ₹7,000–₹25,000 range

### Structure and height

A flat-wall inline stall with a fascia sits at the bottom of the range. Curved forms, cantilevers, overhead rigging, and anything double-height pushes you up fast — more material, more engineering, more build hours on site.

### Materials and finishes

Laminate on MDF is one price. Veneer, glass, metal detailing, textured or backlit surfaces are another. Finish quality is the single most visible difference between a ₹8,000/sqm stall and an ₹18,000/sqm one — visitors can't name it, but they can see it.

### Branding, graphics and lighting

Printed flex is cheap. Fabric lightboxes, routed 3D lettering, and programmed lighting scenes cost more and do more. Lighting in particular punches above its cost — it's often the best upgrade per rupee spent.

### AV and technology

LED walls, interactive screens, and product demo setups are usually the largest single line item after the structure itself. Decide on AV *before* design starts, not after — retrofitting a video wall into a finished design costs more and looks worse.

### Timeline

Rush jobs cost more. A stall booked 8 weeks out gives the workshop time to build efficiently; the same stall booked 3 weeks out means overtime, air freight, and fewer material choices. (More on timelines in our booking guide — link when live.)

### City and venue

The same stall costs different amounts to deliver in different cities — transport, local labour, venue rules and material handling all vary. Ask for the quote to be venue-specific, not generic.

## What the fabrication quote usually does NOT include

Budget these separately, or your total will surprise you:

- **Space rent** — paid to the organizer, typically your single largest cost
- **Organizer charges** — electricity connection, compressed air, water, security deposits
- **Stall services** — hostesses, catering, photography, giveaways
- **Your own costs** — travel, hotels, product shipping, staff time

A stall quote is the *build*; the *show* costs more. If you want one number for everything, ask for turnkey project management — one team, one invoice.

## How to keep cost down without looking cheap

Spend on what visitors touch and see at eye level: the front fascia, the counter, lighting, and one strong focal element. Save on what they don't: back-of-wall finishes, storage room interiors, over-engineered flooring. A disciplined 9 sqm stall with good lighting beats a lazy 36 sqm stall every time — we've built both.

## FAQ

**What does a 3x3 exhibition stall cost in India?**
At ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sqm, a 9 sqm custom stall runs roughly ₹63,000 to ₹2.25 lakh depending on design, materials and finishes.

**Is design charged separately from fabrication?**
The range above covers design and fabrication together. We provide a free 3D concept sketch within 48 hours before you commit.

**Why is the range so wide?**
Because "custom" spans everything from a clean laminate inline stall to a double-height island with LED walls. Structure, finishes, lighting and AV each move the number significantly.

**Do prices differ by city?**
Yes — transport, local labour and venue rules vary. Always get a venue-specific quote.

**What's not included in a stall quote?**
Space rent, organizer utility charges, and your travel and staffing costs. Ask about turnkey management if you want one consolidated number.

**How far in advance should I lock my budget?**
Six to eight weeks before the show is comfortable for a custom stall; ten to twelve weeks if you need a double-decker.
