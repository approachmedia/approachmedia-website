# Exhibitor's Guide to Helipad Exhibition Centre, Gandhinagar

If you exhibit in Gujarat, sooner or later your show lands at Helipad Exhibition Centre in Gandhinagar. Pharma machinery, solar, agriculture, aluminium, food — through the rest of 2026, one venue hosts them all.

This guide covers what's coming up there, and how to plan a stall for it — from a fabrication team based a short drive away in Ahmedabad.

## Why this venue keeps winning shows

Gandhinagar has become Gujarat's exhibition capital, and the Helipad ground is its workhorse venue — large open exhibition space that organizers can configure per show. For exhibitors, that flexibility cuts both ways: the venue adapts to each show's layout, which means **hall plans, stall structures and build rules change from show to show**. The exhibitor manual for your specific show is the only document that tells you your stall's real constraints.

## Planning a stall for a Helipad show

### Book your builder against the show date, not "sometime before"

Count backwards from opening day. A custom stall is comfortable at six to eight weeks; a double-decker needs ten to twelve. For Agri Asia on 11 September, that window has already closed for full custom — for Aluminium Bharat (26 September) it's closing now, and for Khadhya Khurak in December you're in the ideal planning zone today.

### Design for the audience each show brings

The same venue hosts very different buyers. PharmaTech visitors inspect machinery up close — they need demo access and technical conversation space. Agri Asia draws a broad trade audience — visibility and product display carry the day. Aluminium Bharat is a materials industry meeting its supply chain — sample display and meeting space matter most. A stall built for one of these logics at the wrong show wastes money.

### The Gujarat advantage: build local

Stalls delivered from Mumbai or Delhi carry transport, labour-travel and time-buffer costs. Our workshop — 30,000 sq ft of it — is in Ahmedabad, minutes away. Every stall is 100% mock-up tested before dispatch, which matters even more when the venue is close enough that dispatch happens late and setup windows are short.

## FAQ

**Where is Helipad Exhibition Centre?**
In Gandhinagar, Gujarat — the state capital, adjacent to Ahmedabad. Most exhibitors and visitors stay in Ahmedabad and commute.

**What shows run at Helipad Exhibition Centre in 2026?**
Confirmed as of 15 August 2026: PharmaTech Expo (20–22 Aug), Global Solar Expo Gujarat (2–3 Sep), Agri Asia (11–13 Sep), Aluminium Bharat (26–29 Sep) and Khadhya Khurak (16–19 Dec). Check each show's official site for updates.

**How early should I book a stall builder for a Helipad show?**
Six to eight weeks before the show for a custom stall, ten to twelve for a double-decker.

**Do the venue's stall rules stay the same across shows?**
No — the ground is configured per show, so heights, layouts and build rules come from each show's exhibitor manual, not from the venue generally.

**Is Alumax India the same as Aluminium Bharat?**
Aluminium Bharat 2026 is the ALEMAI-organized show carrying the Alumex India legacy forward at Helipad, 26–29 September 2026.
