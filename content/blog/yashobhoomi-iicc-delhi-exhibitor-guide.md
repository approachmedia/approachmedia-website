# Exhibitor's Guide to Yashobhoomi (IICC), New Delhi

Delhi's exhibition map used to have two poles: Pragati Maidan (now Bharat Mandapam) in the centre, and India Expo Mart out in Greater Noida. **Yashobhoomi — the India International Convention & Expo Centre (IICC) in Dwarka — is the newest addition**, and organizers are voting with their bookings: semiconductors, FMCG sourcing, pharma and fenestration shows all land there through the rest of 2026.

If your show is one of them, here's your planning head start.

If you're exhibiting in the pharma cluster, that changes your logistics plan; our dedicated split-venue guide publishes this week.

## What's different about a new venue

### The upside: modern infrastructure

Yashobhoomi was built recently and to convention-grade spec, with metro connectivity to Dwarka and proximity to IGI Airport — a real convenience for international visitors compared with cross-city treks. For shows with overseas buyer traffic (SEMICON, CPHI), that matters to who actually reaches your stall.

### The caution: fewer habits to rely on

At NESCO or India Expo Mart, experienced exhibitors and builders run on years of venue habit. A newer venue means fewer assumptions hold: hall allocation logic, service ordering, delivery access and build-window practice all come fresh from each show's exhibitor manual. The manual is always the law — at a new venue, it's also the only map. Get it to your stall builder the day it arrives, and let them flag what's unusual.

### Dwarka logistics

The venue sits in Dwarka, on Delhi's airport side — the opposite end of NCR from Greater Noida. Factor hotel choice and material delivery routes accordingly, especially for the split-venue pharma weeks in November when some teams will shuttle between both venues.

## FAQ

**What is Yashobhoomi?**
The India International Convention & Expo Centre (IICC) in Dwarka, New Delhi — Delhi's newest major convention and exhibition venue.

**Which shows run at Yashobhoomi in 2026?**
Confirmed as of 15 August 2026: SEMICON India (17–19 Sep), CMPL Expo Delhi (23–24 Oct), CPHI India (23–25 Nov) and Zak Doors & Windows Expo (10–12 Dec).

**Is CPHI India at Yashobhoomi or India Expo Mart?**
CPHI India 2026 is at Yashobhoomi; PMEC India runs at India Expo Mart in the same week — a split-venue format across Delhi NCR.

**How early should I book a stall builder for a Yashobhoomi show?**
Six to eight weeks before opening for custom; ten to twelve for a double-decker. For the November and December shows, book now.

**Are stall rules at Yashobhoomi fixed by the venue?**
No — each show's organizer sets heights, approvals and build windows in its exhibitor manual. At a newer venue, read it especially closely; fewer old habits apply.
