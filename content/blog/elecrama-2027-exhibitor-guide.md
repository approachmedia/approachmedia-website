# ELECRAMA 2027: Exhibiting at the Electrical Industry's Biennial Flagship

Some shows are annual appointments; ELECRAMA is a cycle. **The 17th edition of IEEMA's electrical and power equipment flagship runs 20–24 February 2027 — five days at India Expo Mart, Greater Noida** — and because the show returns only every other year, what you build there represents your brand for the full cycle. Exhibitors budget accordingly, which raises the floor's ambient standard the way flagships always do.

Published eleven weeks out: every option is still on the table, including the two-storey one.

## Stall logic for the power industry

**Heavy, live, or both.** Transformers, switchgear, panels — ELECRAMA exhibits are heavy machinery with the added layer of electrical demonstration. That means the full arrives-first discipline (declared weights, machine-before-stall sequencing, floor loading) plus genuinely engineered power planning: demo loads declared early, energised displays designed with safety clearance, and the stall's own electrical plan kept scrupulously separate from the demo circuits. At an electrical show, your wiring discipline is on display professionally.

**The cycle argument for quality.** A biennial flagship compresses two years of brand impression into five days. Finish quality, lighting execution and a composed photographic angle aren't vanity here — the photos work for 24 months. (The same logic that made the [Eleanor award stall](/blog/award-winning-exhibition-stall-design-eleanor-aluminium-bharat-2026) worth its restraint.)

**Utility and institutional buyers need rooms.** ELECRAMA's audience includes utilities, EPCs and government procurement — committee conversations, not aisle chats. Enclosed or semi-enclosed meeting capacity, documentation at hand, and a team roster that survives five days ([the endurance rules](/blog/imtex-2027-exhibitor-guide) in their five-day form).

**The venue's fourth big show in eight weeks.** IndusFood, IFEX and ELECRAMA stack at India Expo Mart between January and late February ([venue guide](/blog/india-expo-mart-greater-noida-exhibitor-guide)) — build slots, hotels and local services all feel it. Book against that congestion, not just your own timeline.

## FAQ

**When and where is ELECRAMA 2027?**
20–24 February 2027 — five days — at India Expo Mart, Greater Noida. The 17th edition, organized by IEEMA.

**Can equipment be energised at the stall?**
Live demonstration is standard at ELECRAMA within the manual's rules — declare demo loads early and design safety clearances in; approvals are structured and deadlines firm.

**Why do exhibitors invest more at ELECRAMA than annual shows?**
It's biennial — the stall and its photographs represent the brand for a two-year cycle, so quality compounds.
