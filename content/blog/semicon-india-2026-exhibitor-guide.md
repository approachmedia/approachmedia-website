# SEMICON India 2026: Stand Planning for a Precision Industry

Semiconductors are India's most-watched industrial story, and SEMICON India is where that story meets the trade-show floor — **17–19 September 2026 at Yashobhoomi (IICC), Dwarka, New Delhi**, organized by SEMI with the India Semiconductor Mission.

An industry built on precision judges its exhibitors the same way. Here's what that means for your stand.

## Who walks this floor — and what they expect

SEMICON India draws the full semiconductor supply chain: fab and OSAT players, equipment and materials suppliers, design houses, plus government and institutional delegations — the show runs jointly with the India Semiconductor Mission, and official visits are part of its rhythm. Your stand may host an engineer at 11 am, a procurement head at 2 pm and a state-delegation walkthrough at 4. Design for all three.

## The stand logic for a precision industry

### Precision is the aesthetic

In this industry, visual noise reads as process noise. The stands that earn credibility at technology shows are controlled: disciplined geometry, flawless finishes, restrained palettes, engineered lighting. This is where fabrication quality is exposed — a wavy panel or rough joint contradicts everything a semiconductor company sells. It's exactly why we mock-up test 100% of builds before dispatch: at a precision show, "almost right" is visibly wrong.

### Demo-first, brochure-last

Working demos out-convert everything else at technology shows. Plan the demo stations first — power provisioning, screen sightlines, network needs, equipment security overnight — and let the stand form follow. AV is usually the largest line item after structure at tech stands; decide it before design starts, not after. (Our [AV & technology integration](/services/av-technology-integration) service exists for precisely this.)

### The delegation moment

Because ISM co-organizes, delegation walkthroughs happen. If a walkthrough reaches your stand, you get ninety seconds of concentrated attention: one clear headline about what you do for India's semiconductor build-out, visible from the aisle, does more in that moment than any brochure rack.

### Meeting space earns its square metres

Supply-chain deals are technical conversations under NDA instincts. A semi-enclosed meeting room — acoustically reasonable, with a screen for slides — is worth sacrificing display space for.

## Timeline check

Publishing three weeks before opening: the comfortable 6–8 week custom window has passed. A disciplined, tightly-scoped custom build — clean structure, demo stations, meeting space, engineered lighting — remains achievable with immediate scope freeze. Complex or double-height builds should wait for your next show. If you're reading this for 2027 planning instead, you're perfectly early: lock your builder 6–8 weeks out.

## FAQ

**When and where is SEMICON India 2026?**
17–19 September 2026 at Yashobhoomi (IICC), Dwarka, New Delhi. Organized by SEMI jointly with the India Semiconductor Mission.

**What should a semiconductor-industry stand prioritize?**
Working demos, disciplined design language, and a proper meeting space — in that order. Precision industries judge fabrication quality as a proxy for process quality.

**Is there still time to build a custom stand for SEMICON India 2026?**
