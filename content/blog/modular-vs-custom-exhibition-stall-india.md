# Modular vs Custom Exhibition Stalls: Which Is Right for You in 2026?

One of the most common questions we hear from exhibitors is: "Should we go modular or custom?"

A few years ago, the answer was simple. Modular meant cheap and basic. Custom meant premium but expensive. In 2026, that line has blurred. Modular systems now look far better, and many leading brands use a **hybrid approach** that combines both.

Here's a clear comparison to help you decide.

## What is a custom exhibition stall?

A custom stall is designed and built specifically for one show and one brand, usually using plywood, MDF, paint, laminates and printed graphics. It is often dismantled and discarded after the event.

**Advantages:**

- Complete design freedom
- Unique shapes, heights and architectural features
- Strong "wow" factor

**Limitations:**

- Higher cost per show
- Longer production time (often six to eight weeks for complex designs)
- Mostly single-use, creating more waste

## What is a modular exhibition stall?

A modular stall uses a reusable system of frames (usually aluminium), panels and fabric graphics. The same kit can be reconfigured for different sizes and layouts.

**Advantages:**

- Lower cost across multiple shows
- Faster setup and dismantling
- Easy to update with new graphics
- More sustainable

**Limitations:**

- Some design constraints
- Can look generic if not designed well
- Storage and logistics need planning

## Modular vs custom: a quick comparison

| Factor | Custom stall | Modular stall |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront cost | Higher per show | Higher first time, lower after |
| Cost over 3–5 shows | High | Much lower |
| Design freedom | Complete | Good, with some limits |
| Setup time | Longer | Faster |
| Reusability | Low | High |
| Sustainability | Lower | Higher |
| Best for | One major flagship show | Brands doing several shows a year |

Industry estimates suggest modular systems can reduce total cost of ownership by 40 to 60% over five years.

## The hybrid approach: why it's winning in 2026

Many exhibitors now choose a **modular base with custom "hero" elements**. For example:

- A reusable modular frame for walls and structure
- A custom feature arch, product display or demo theatre as the centrepiece
- Premium finishes and lighting on key surfaces

This gives you:

- The cost and sustainability benefits of modular
- The unique look and impact of custom
- The flexibility to adapt the stall for different show sizes

One common criticism of modular stalls is that they lack a clear focal point. The hybrid approach solves exactly that.

## Which option suits your business?

**Choose custom if:**

- You exhibit at one or two major shows a year
- You're launching a major product or rebranding
- You need complex architecture, double-decker structures or unusual shapes

**Choose modular if:**

- You exhibit at four or more shows a year
- You exhibit across different cities and stall sizes
- Budget control and speed matter most

**Choose hybrid if:**

- You exhibit regularly but still want a premium presence
- You want to reduce long-term cost without looking generic
- Sustainability is a priority for your brand

## Questions to ask your stall partner

1. How many shows can this system be reused for?
2. Can it adapt from, for example, 9 sq m to 36 sq m?
3. Where will it be stored between shows, and at what cost?
4. Which parts are custom, and which are reusable?
5. What does the three-year cost look like, not just the first show?
