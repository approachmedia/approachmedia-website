# Gulfood 2027 Is in March Now: The Indian Exporter's Stand Guide

Check your calendar before you book anything: **Gulfood 2027 runs 15–19 March — not its traditional February slot — across both Dubai World Trade Centre and Dubai Exhibition Centre at Expo City.** For the Indian food exporters who treat Gulfood as the year's biggest international outing — and India is consistently among the show's largest exhibitor contingents, pavilions included — the date move and the two-venue footprint are the two planning facts of the season.

Published eleven weeks out, this guide sits exactly on the international three-month line: the right week to move.

## What the two changes mean

**The March move reshuffles your quarter.** Exporters who ran Gulfood in February and a Europe show in March now face a different sequence — and the stand build conversation moves with it. If [IndusFood in January](/blog/indusfood-2027-exhibitor-guide) is also on your calendar, the two shows now bracket your Q1 instead of colliding with it: one design language, two outings, better value from both.

**Two venues means confirm before you plan.** Like [WHX Dubai](/blog/whx-dubai-2027-indian-exhibitors-guide), Gulfood 2027 spans the two sites we compared in [DWTC vs Dubai Exhibition Centre](/blog/dwtc-vs-dubai-exhibition-centre-guide) — opposite ends of the city, separate logistics, separate build operations. Your sector's hall allocation decides your hotel cluster, delivery route and build rules; get it confirmed early and hand it to your stand partner the same day.

## The Gulfood stand brief

The export-food logic from our [IndusFood guide](/blog/indusfood-2027-exhibitor-guide) at international scale: reach made visible (markets served, retail-ready packaging on shelf-style display), certifications in the headline zone (they're the importer's gate), and tasting as designed infrastructure — service counters, declared utilities, queue space. Add the Gulfood-specific layer: five days, enormous halls, and buyers scheduling by country pavilion — so if you're *outside* the India pavilion, your stand carries the whole recognition job alone; if you're *inside* it, differentiation within the contingent is the game ([the CPHI Milan argument](/blog/cphi-milan-2026-indian-exhibitors-guide), food edition).

## FAQ

**When is Gulfood 2027?**
15–19 March 2027 — moved from its traditional February window. Verify dates on gulfood.com before booking travel.

**Which venue will my stand be at?**
Gulfood 2027 spans both DWTC and Dubai Exhibition Centre at Expo City — your hall allocation determines the site, and with it logistics and rules. Confirm early.

**Should Indian exporters exhibit inside or outside the India pavilion?**
Both work with different briefs: inside, differentiate within the contingent; outside, carry full brand recognition alone. The stand design follows the choice.

**When should the stand conversation start?**
