# REI Expo + The Battery Show India 2026: One Venue, Two Shows, One Plan

On 22–24 October 2026, India Expo Mart in Greater Noida hosts two of the country's most important clean-energy events at once: **Renewable Energy India (REI) Expo** and **The Battery Show India** — both Informa Markets shows, running on the same dates at the same venue.

For exhibitors, that's not trivia. It changes where you book, what your stand says, and how far your budget goes. And unlike our recent show guides, this one comes with good news about timing: opening day is eight weeks away, which puts you **exactly in the ideal booking window today**.

## Which show is yours? (Sometimes it's both)

- **Solar modules, EPC, inverters, hydrogen, wind-adjacent** → REI is your floor.
- **Cells, packs, BMS, charging, battery materials and manufacturing equipment** → The Battery Show.
- **Storage players sit in the overlap** — an ESS company can justify either. The deciding question is who signs your purchase orders: project developers and EPCs (REI) or OEMs and pack manufacturers (Battery Show).
- **Exhibiting at both?** Some supply-chain companies do. If so, resist building two unrelated stands: one design language, scaled to two spaces, cuts cost and doubles brand recall for visitors who walk both floors.

## Planning the stand for a clean-energy crowd

### Show the hardware, prove the numbers

Clean-energy buyers are engineers and project-finance people in the same body. The stand needs the physical product — panels, cells, racks, chargers — plus the performance story presented legibly. A product wall with clear spec communication outperforms abstract brand graphics at every energy show.

### Energy shows reward literal brightness

It sounds obvious, but lighting is the most underused weapon at renewable shows: a bright, white, energy-confident stand communicates the category promise. It's also the best cost-per-impact upgrade in the entire budget — a point we make in detail in the [cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india).

### Two-show visibility

With parallel events, aisle traffic includes people who didn't come for you. A stand that states its category in three seconds — one headline, visible from distance — recruits from the neighbouring show's overflow. Height and corner positions are worth more this October than usual.

## FAQ

**Are REI Expo and The Battery Show India the same event?**
No — two separate Informa Markets shows that run on the same dates (22–24 October 2026) at the same venue, India Expo Mart, Greater Noida.

**Which show should an energy-storage company choose?**
Follow your buyers: project developers and EPCs → REI; OEMs and battery-pack manufacturers → The Battery Show. Companies serving both sometimes exhibit at both.

**When should I book my stall builder for these shows?**
Now. At eight weeks out you're in the ideal 6–8 week custom window; a double-decker needs the decision immediately (10–12 weeks).

**What does a stall at India Expo Mart cost?**
The India range applies: ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sq metre for custom design and fabrication depending on design, plus venue-specific logistics. Full breakdown in the [cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india).

**What else runs at India Expo Mart in late 2026?**
