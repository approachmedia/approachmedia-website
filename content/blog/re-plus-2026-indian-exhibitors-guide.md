# RE+ 2026: A Booth Guide for Indian Clean-Energy Exporters

For Indian solar module makers, cell manufacturers, storage players and EV-supply companies eyeing the American market, one event dominates the calendar: **RE+ — North America's largest clean-energy show — running 16–19 November 2026 at the Las Vegas Convention Center**, presented by RE+ Events and powered by SEIA and SEPA.

Exhibiting in the US is a different discipline from exhibiting in India — different vocabulary (it's a "booth", not a "stall"), different rules, different costs. Here's the honest orientation, published seven weeks out — late for first-timers to start from zero, right on time for exhibitors finalizing plans.

## What changes when you exhibit in the US

### The labour model is different — plan for it, don't fight it

At major US convention venues, booth installation, material handling and services run through the venue's designated contractors and regulated labour arrangements. Material handling from dock to booth — "drayage" in US show vocabulary — is a paid, scheduled service, not something your team improvises. None of this is a problem if your booth is designed for it: structures that assemble predictably, documented to US practice, with services ordered through the exhibitor kit by its deadlines. The exhibitor kit is the US equivalent of the Indian exhibitor manual — and it rules everything.

### The design language rewards clarity

US clean-energy buyers move fast through enormous halls. The booths that work are legible from the aisle — one headline, product front and centre, spec access immediate. The advice we gave for [REI Expo and The Battery Show India](/blog/rei-expo-battery-show-india-2026-guide) applies doubled: category clarity in three seconds, meetings bookable in advance, and lighting that makes hardware look engineered.

### Your India credibility is an asset — present it deliberately

Indian module and cell capacity is a live topic in the US market. A booth that states manufacturing scale, certifications relevant to the US buyer, and delivery capability plainly does more than one that leads with generic brand language. As at [CPHI Milan](/blog/cphi-milan-2026-indian-exhibitors-guide), the goal is standing apart from the exporters you'll be compared against directly.

### Build options from India

The same three paths we outlined for [Dubai](/blog/exhibition-stand-cost-dubai-indian-exhibitors) apply, with US specifics: hire a US exhibit house directly; design in India and deliver through a partner with international execution; or a hybrid where reusable brand elements travel and the structure is executed to US practice locally. Shipping a full custom booth from India to Las Vegas is rarely the right answer once freight, customs and the labour model are priced in — ask hard questions of anyone proposing it as the default.

## The timeline, stated plainly

Seven weeks out. If your booth program is already moving — finalize scope now, order services by the exhibitor-kit deadlines, and lock your on-site schedule. If you're starting from zero today: a full custom build is no longer realistic; a well-executed package booth with strong graphics and lighting, or an upgraded space within a pavilion, is the honest route — and your 2027 international calendar should start three or more months out, a rule we apply to every overseas show.

## FAQ

**When and where is RE+ 2026?**
16–19 November 2026 at the Las Vegas Convention Center — confirmed on the organizer's official future-dates page.

**Can an Indian company design its RE+ booth in India?**
Yes — design in India with delivery through a partner executing to US venue practice is a standard model. Direct US exhibit houses and hybrids are the alternatives.

**What is "drayage"?**
US show vocabulary for material handling between the loading dock and your booth — a scheduled, paid venue service ordered through the exhibitor kit, not something your own team does.

**Is seven weeks enough to prepare for RE+?**
Enough to finalize a program already in motion; not enough to start a custom build from zero. Package booths with strong graphics are the honest late option.

**Should we exhibit at REI Expo in India or RE+ in the US?**
Different buyers: REI reaches Indian developers and EPCs; RE+ reaches the North American market. Exporters serious about the US treat RE+ as its own program, not an REI add-on.
