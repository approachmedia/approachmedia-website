Some shows are institutions. **Khadhya Khurak — Gujarat's food processing, packaging and hospitality expo — runs its 23rd edition on 16–19 December 2026 at the Helipad Ground (Gate No. 9), Gandhinagar.** Twenty-three editions means two decades of Gujarat's food industry treating this as its year-end meeting point: processors, packagers, equipment makers, hospitality suppliers and the trade that buys from all of them.

## Stall logic for a food-industry crowd

### Hygiene-visual design, again — because it works

We made this argument for [Anuga FoodTec India](/blog/anuga-foodtec-india-2026-exhibitor-guide) and it holds at every food show: buyers in this industry read bright, clean-lined, flawlessly finished stalls as a proxy for the standard your plant runs to. Light palettes, ordered displays, no visual clutter.

### Four days, regional intensity

A 23rd-edition regional institution draws its whole ecosystem — including repeat visitors who come more than once across the four days. Like the six-day marathon logic in our [India ITME guide](/blog/india-itme-2026-exhibitor-guide) at smaller scale: spec finishes for duration, stock consumables for four days, and brief your team for returning faces.

### Tasting and demo culture

Food expos run on sampling. If your stall will serve or demonstrate, plan the service line early — power, counters at serving height, waste handling, and queue space that doesn't block your own displays. Utilities are requested through the organizer; declare them when you book, not on build day.

### December at the Helipad Ground

This is an open exhibition ground configured per show (the full venue picture is in our [Helipad Exhibition Centre guide](/blog/helipad-exhibition-centre-gandhinagar-guide)) — December's mild weather is the pleasant face of it; per-show layouts mean your stall's real constraints come from the exhibitor manual, as always.

16–19 December 2026 at the Helipad Ground (Gate No. 9), Gandhinagar — the 23rd edition, organized by Khimashia Associates.

Yes, subject to the organizer's rules — declare power, water and service needs when booking, and design the serving line into the stall rather than improvising it.
