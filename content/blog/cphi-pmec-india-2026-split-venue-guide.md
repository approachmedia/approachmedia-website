# CPHI & PMEC India 2026: Two Venues, One Pharma Week — Plan Accordingly

Most exhibitors say "CPHI-PMEC" as one word, because for years it has been one event. In 2026, it isn't — not physically. **CPHI India runs at Yashobhoomi (IICC), Dwarka, New Delhi on 23–25 November, while PMEC India runs at India Expo Mart, Greater Noida on 24–26 November.** Same organizer, same week, two venues at opposite ends of Delhi NCR, with a two-day overlap.

If you're exhibiting, sourcing, or doing both, this changes your November. Here's the plan.

## What the split means in practice

### Pick your primary floor by what you sell

Ingredients, intermediates, services → CPHI at Yashobhoomi. Machinery, equipment, packaging lines → PMEC at India Expo Mart. For most exhibitors the choice is made by their catalogue. The harder calls are companies spanning both — equipment makers with consumables, or CDMOs courting both audiences.

### The cross-venue visitor is real — and rushed

During the two overlap days, serious buyers will attempt both venues. Dwarka to Greater Noida is a long NCR crossing; anyone doing both in a day arrives at the second venue tired and behind schedule. Two consequences for your stand: make the first five metres do the work (a rushed visitor triages brutally), and make meetings bookable in advance so cross-venue visitors commit a time slot instead of "dropping by".

### One brand, two-venue presence?

Exhibiting at both venues doubles cost but not necessarily impact. The disciplined alternative: one full stand at your primary venue, and a focused presence at the other — smaller footprint, one message, meeting-first layout. If you do take two stands, insist on one design language: the buyer who sees you in Dwarka on Monday should recognize you instantly in Greater Noida on Wednesday.

### Logistics double quietly

Two venues mean two exhibitor manuals, two sets of submission deadlines, two build windows, two delivery routes — even for one company. This is precisely the situation [turnkey project management](/services/turnkey-project-management) exists for: one team owning both venues' paperwork, builds and deadlines, one invoice.

## FAQ

**Are CPHI India and PMEC India at the same venue in 2026?**
No — CPHI India is at Yashobhoomi (Dwarka, New Delhi), 23–25 November; PMEC India is at India Expo Mart (Greater Noida), 24–26 November. They overlap on 24–25 November.

**Which show should my company exhibit at?**
Ingredients and services → CPHI; machinery and equipment → PMEC. Companies spanning both should choose a primary venue and consider a focused secondary presence rather than two full stands.

**Can visitors attend both venues in one day?**
The overlap days make it possible but demanding — the venues sit at opposite ends of Delhi NCR. Check the official site for inter-venue transport arrangements, and book meetings in advance.

**When should I book my stall builder?**
Now — ten weeks out is the ideal window for custom builds, and the last comfortable moment to decide on a double-decker.

**What does a stall at these shows cost?**
The India range applies: ₹7,000–₹25,000 per sq metre custom, depending on design, quoted venue-specifically — details in our [cost guide](/blog/exhibition-stall-design-cost-india). A two-venue presence is quoted as one project.
