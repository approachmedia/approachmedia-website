# Arab Health Is Now WHX Dubai: The 2027 Exhibitor's Guide for Indian Companies

If your 2027 export calendar says "Arab Health", update it. The Middle East's biggest healthcare show has been rebranded: it now runs as **WHX Dubai, 25–28 January 2027**, organized by Informa Markets — and it spans **two venues at once: Dubai Exhibition Centre at Expo City and Dubai World Trade Centre.** Its laboratory sister show, formerly Medlab Middle East, is now **WHX Labs Dubai**, co-located on the same dates rather than running separately in February.

For Indian medical-device, pharma and healthcare exporters — among the largest contingents at this show for years — that's three planning changes in one: a new name, a two-site footprint, and a combined health-plus-labs week. Here's the stand-planning read, published eleven weeks out, right in the window to act.

## What the changes mean for your stand

### The rebrand is a recall problem — and an opportunity

Buyers will say "Arab Health" for years; your pre-show outreach should say both names. On the stand itself, nothing changes about the job: certifications for the Gulf buyer's markets, export range legible from the aisle, meeting space for delegation conversations — the export-show logic we laid out for [iPHEX](/blog/iphex-2026-exhibitor-guide) transplanted to Dubai.

### Two venues means know your hall before you design

Which venue — and which hall — your stand sits in now shapes everything from visitor profile to delivery route. The two sites sit at opposite ends of Dubai, with different build rules and contractor logistics. Confirm your allocation early, and hand it to your stand partner the same day. (The same split-venue discipline we mapped for [CPHI & PMEC India](/blog/cphi-pmec-india-2026-split-venue-guide) applies: divide the logistics, unify the brand.)

### The labs merger widens the aisle traffic

With WHX Labs on the same dates, diagnostics and lab-equipment buyers share the week with hospital and device buyers. If your range spans both, say so on the fascia; if it doesn't, category clarity keeps the wrong half of the traffic from consuming your team's time.

## FAQ

**Is WHX Dubai the same as Arab Health?**
Yes — Arab Health has been rebranded WHX Dubai from the 2027 edition, with Medlab Middle East becoming WHX Labs Dubai, co-located on the same January dates.

**When and where is WHX Dubai 2027?**
25–28 January 2027, across two venues: Dubai Exhibition Centre at Expo City and Dubai World Trade Centre.

**Which venue will my stand be in?**
Allocation depends on your sector and booking — confirm with the organizer early, because venue and hall determine build rules, logistics and audience flow.

**Can an Indian company build its WHX stand through an Indian designer?**
Yes — design in India, delivery through a partner with established UAE execution is a standard model; venue contractor registration and approvals are handled within it.

**How early should I start?**
Three months or more for international shows — meaning now, for a late-January show. Late starts narrow to shell-scheme upgrades and pavilion packages.
