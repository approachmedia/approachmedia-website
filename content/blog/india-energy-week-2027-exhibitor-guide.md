# India Energy Week 2027: Exhibiting in Kolkata

**India Energy Week — the government-anchored energy flagship organized by FIPI under the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas — runs 28–31 January 2027 at the New Town Convention Centre, Kolkata.** Four days, the oil, gas and wider energy ecosystem, ministerial presence, and international delegations: the formal-flagship profile we've described at [Bharat Mandapam](/blog/bharat-mandapam-exhibitor-guide), transplanted east.

For most exhibitors the headline is the city: Kolkata hosts fewer mega-shows than Delhi NCR or Mumbai, which makes venue habits scarcer and planning discipline more valuable. Published five weeks out — tight, honest window below.

## Stall logic for a government-anchored energy flagship

**The delegation moment, again.** Ministerial and international walkthroughs are part of this show's rhythm — the one-headline rule applies in full: what you do for India's energy system, readable from the aisle in three seconds, with everything else subordinate.

**Scale models and story walls carry the sector.** Refinery units, pipelines and offshore assets don't visit exhibitions; precision models, flow graphics and strong AV do their talking — the [Windergy playbook](/blog/windergy-india-2026-exhibitor-guide) for products too big to bring, in its heaviest industry.

**Institutional meetings need rooms.** PSUs, EPCs and government buyers hold structured conversations: enclosed meeting space with documentation isn't a luxury line here, it's the conversion mechanism.

**A less-worn venue means the manual is everything.** With fewer mega-show habits to lean on in Kolkata, the exhibitor manual and early organizer coordination carry more weight than usual — deadlines, build windows, delivery access all verified rather than assumed ([the approvals discipline](/blog/stand-design-approval-process-india)). Our delivery model is built for exactly this: fabricate and 100% mock-up test in the Ahmedabad workshop, dispatch finished, assemble in the window — the venue's distance from our workshop changes the truck route, not the standard. Kolkata is one of our listed service cities.

## The timeline, plainly

Five weeks out is past the comfortable 6–8 week line: a disciplined custom build is achievable with an immediate decision and frozen scope; complex structures should wait for the next edition. The fabrication falls across the new year — book this week and the calendar still works.
