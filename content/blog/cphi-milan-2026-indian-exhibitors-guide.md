# CPHI Milan 2026: A Stand Guide for Indian Pharma Exporters

For Indian pharma, CPHI is the export calendar's main event — the global sourcing platform where API manufacturers, formulators, excipient suppliers and CDMOs meet the world's buyers. In 2026 the European edition lands at **Fiera Milano, 6–8 October**, organized by Informa Markets.

Indian companies exhibit at CPHI in force — which is exactly the problem this guide addresses. When hundreds of exhibitors from your own country share the floor, your stand's job is not "represent India". It's *stand apart from the companies a buyer will compare you against directly*.

## The Indian exhibitor's CPHI problem

At iPHEX, you're an Indian exporter meeting visiting buyers. At CPHI Milan, you're one of a very large Indian contingent — and European, Chinese, and American competitors are in the same halls. Three consequences for the stand:

### Differentiate within the contingent, not just the industry

Buyers walking a CPHI hall triage fast. A stand that leads with a generic "WHO-GDP | US FDA | EU-GMP" checklist looks like every neighbour. Lead instead with the one thing a buyer should remember — your niche molecule class, your capacity milestone, your delivery record — stated in aisle-readable type, with certifications as supporting cast.

### European build standards are visible

Fiera Milano hosts some of the best stand construction in the world; the ambient quality bar is high. A stall that would look sharp at an Indian venue can look budget next to German and Italian builds. Finish quality, lighting design and material choices carry more competitive weight here than at any Indian show — budget accordingly.

### Meetings are booked before the show

CPHI runs on pre-scheduled meetings. Your stand is the venue for a diary that fills weeks in advance — so meeting capacity (two conversations in parallel, acoustic reasonableness, refreshments, storage) is the core design spec, not an amenity.

## FAQ

**When and where is CPHI Milan 2026?**
6–8 October 2026 at Fiera Milano (Rho), Milan — the European edition of CPHI, organized by Informa Markets.

**Can an Indian company build its CPHI stand through an Indian designer?**
Yes — design in India with delivery through an international execution process is standard. Venue rules require working with registered contractors and organizer approvals; a turnkey partner manages that.

**Is one month enough to organize a CPHI Milan stand?**
It's late for custom. A sharply-executed shell upgrade or pavilion package is the honest route now; for the next edition — or for CPHI & PMEC India in November — start now.

**How should an Indian exhibitor stand out at CPHI?**
Lead with one memorable specific (niche, capacity, track record) instead of a generic certification wall, and match the European finish-quality bar — the ambient standard at Fiera Milano is high.

**What about CPHI India?**
CPHI & PMEC India 2026 run 23–26 November across two Delhi-NCR venues — Yashobhoomi and India Expo Mart. The booking window for those is ideal right now.
